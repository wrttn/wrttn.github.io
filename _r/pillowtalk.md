---
title: Pillowtalk
author: Ursula K. Le Guin
layout: default
---

Are you asleep? We lie here hearing
the rain in the dark. I want to ask you,
Why is it all so strange?

*I'm sleeping and dreaming of the rain
in the dark, in the dark ground.
I'm ranging out, ranging far.*

You're lying here beside me
as you've always lain.
But it all keeps changing, changing.

Is it your voice or the rain
I hear like somebody weeping?
*Lie easy, love. We're out of danger.*
