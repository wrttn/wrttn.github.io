## Ingredients

Dressing
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 2-3 tbsp fresh orange juice
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2-3 tbsp toasted sesame oil

Vegetables:

- Thinly sliced red or green cabbage, carrots, scallions and cilantro
