## Ingredients

Dry Rub

- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tsp black pepper (freshly cracked if possible)
- 1 tsp paprika (for color and smokiness)
- 1 tsp sumac (bright citrusy tang)
- 1 tsp coriander seed (lightly toasted, then ground)
- 1/2 tsp cumin seed (lightly toasted, then ground)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes (adjust to your heat level)

Web Mop / Glaze

- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard (you’ve got Maille — perfect)
- 1 tbsp cider vinegar
- 1 tsp sumac
- 1 tsp honey or brown sugar (if you have it, balances acidity)
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tbsp olive oil (or any neutral oil)
